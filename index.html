About SalafiScope Task

A calm, focused task manager for planning your day by typing or by speaking. Version 1 · your data stays on this device. Voice recordings are never saved — only the resulting tasks. Reminders fire while SalafiScope Task is open (in a tab or as an installed app) — a true alarm that works with the app fully closed would need a push server, which this static app doesn't have.
